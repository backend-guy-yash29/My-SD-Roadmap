# Interview Prep

> Company focus areas, behavioral preparation and timed mock interviews.

## Company Focus Areas

- Google: Large-Scale Infrastructure, Storage and Search
- Meta: News Feeds, Messaging and Social Graphs
- Amazon: Commerce, Distributed Workflows and Operational Excellence
- Uber: Geospatial Systems, Marketplaces and Real-Time Streaming
- Netflix: Streaming, Recommendations and CDNs

## Behavioral

- STAR Format
- Story Bank of 8–10 Stories from Real Work
- Amazon Leadership Principles
- Project Deep Dive
- Why Are You Leaving and Why This Company
