# Evaluation Metrics

> Scoring classifiers, regressors and ranked results.

## Classification Metrics

- Confusion Matrix
- Accuracy
- Precision
- Recall
- F1 Score
- ROC Curve and AUC

## Regression Metrics

- MAE, MSE and RMSE
- R-Squared

## Ranking and Retrieval Metrics

- Precision@K
- Recall@K
- Hit Rate
- Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)
- Mean Average Precision (MAP)
- NDCG
